Travel Well began with a simple belief: the best trips are the ones where you reach out, notice the beauty around you and stay curious enough to try the unfamiliar plate, the unfamiliar song, the unfamiliar street.

This Journal is where those moments will live. Expect short, honest stories about kind strangers, new flavours, music and culture, and the quiet scenery that is easy to miss when you are rushing.

## What you will find here

- **Stories from the road.** Humble, first-hand moments that encourage reaching out to others while you travel.
- **Travel notes for her, for him and for the two of you.** Practical thoughts on packing light, looking and feeling well on the road, and what makes a trip easier.
- **Packing notes.** Plain guidance on carry-on and liquids rules, with the date we last checked them, because rules change.

## How we write

We share what we have actually seen and done, and we say plainly when we are passing along something we have not tried ourselves.

Thank you for being here. Pack light, stay curious and say hello to someone new.
