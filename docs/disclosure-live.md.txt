Travel Well is supported by affiliate links. If you click a link to a retailer or brand and make a purchase, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra.

## What this means

- **Recommendations come first.** We choose products because they are practical, well made and suit modern travel, not because of a commission.
- **We say what we know.** When we have used a product ourselves, we say so. When we have not, we say that too.
- **Prices and availability** are set by the retailer and can change. Check the retailer's page before you buy.
- **Rules change.** Airline and airport rules, such as liquids limits, are checked on the date shown on each page. Check with your airline and airport before you fly.
- **Voltage and safety.** For electric products we share the maker's stated voltage range. Always check the product label and manual before plugging in abroad.

## Programs

We may take part in affiliate programs run by retailers and affiliate networks. The specific programs we use will be listed here as they are approved.

## Images

Many images on this site are AI-generated illustrations. They are not photographs of real customers and do not represent real endorsements.
