In a few months I'm heading to Brazil: a week in Manaus, then two weeks in Porto Alegre. Before I go, I'm doing something simple every day. I practice speaking Portuguese for fifteen to thirty minutes.

My Portuguese is somewhere in the middle: good enough to follow along, not yet good enough to relax. Some days the words come easily. Other days I'm hunting for them, and that is exactly the point.

I don't want to arrive as a visitor who points at a menu. I want to say good morning, ask a question and listen to the answer. If you're traveling somewhere new, even a few weeks of daily practice changes how you're met, and how much you notice.

What's one phrase you always learn before you land?
